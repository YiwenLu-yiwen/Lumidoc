Harbor Studio — Customer support guide

This is fictional sample data for the Lumidoc walkthrough.

Sam Rivera owns support preparation for the Harbor Studio website launch.
Support coverage is Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 in the studio's local time.
The first response target is one business day.

Customers can cancel a booking without a fee up to 24 hours before its start.
Requests inside that window are reviewed by the support team individually.
The team must confirm the booking identifier before making a change.

For a website outage, Sam records the incident and contacts Leo Park.
Maya Chen decides whether to delay the launch if the booking flow is affected.
During the first week, the team reviews open incidents at 09:30 each morning.

Before 15 April 2027, Sam must approve this guide, publish the contact details,
and confirm the coverage schedule. The team rehearses an outage on 8 April 2027.
