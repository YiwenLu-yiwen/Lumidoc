Harbor Studio — Launch brief

This is fictional sample data for the Lumidoc walkthrough.

Harbor Studio is preparing a public launch of its booking website on 15 April 2027.
Maya Chen is the launch owner. Leo Park owns the website implementation, and
Sam Rivera owns customer support preparation.

Before launch, the team must complete three checks:
1. Test the booking flow on desktop and mobile.
2. Publish the cancellation policy and contact information.
3. Approve the customer support guide and assign the first week of coverage.

The team reviews readiness every Tuesday at 10:00. Maya makes the final go/no-go
decision after all three checks are complete. The rehearsal is on 8 April 2027.

The launch budget is 2,400 fictional units: 1,200 for design, 800 for testing,
and 400 for customer support training. No real company or individual is described.
